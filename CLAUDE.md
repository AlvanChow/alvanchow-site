This repo is alvanchow.com, a single-file portfolio site (index.html).

Style rules, always enforced:
- Font: Frank Ruhl Libre only. Body weight 300, titles 500. No italics, no new fonts.
- word-spacing: -0.04em stays on the body.
- No em dashes. No one-word sentences.
- Captions: one line, under 10 words, no trailing period.
- Entry format: plain title, then optional gray link slug with an arrow, then lowercase status floated right.
- Link slugs: real domains display as themselves; ugly auto-generated URLs display as one word (visit, play, App Store).
- Statuses: "live · beta" (green) for web products, "live on the App Store" (green) for approved apps, "App Store, coming soon", "in development", "prototype", "seeking design partners".
- Cream palette (#FAF7F0), hairline rules, no boxes or shadows.
- Sections: Productivity Tools, Consumer Apps, Moonshots, Gaming in a 2-column grid, then Writing full-width.

After every edit: commit with a short message and push to main.

Never schedule check-ins, reminders, or recurring triggers, and never subscribe to PR activity. Do the work, push, and end the turn.
